# Executive Summary

## Project
E-Commerce Sales & Customer Performance Analytics

## Scope
This report analyses the supplied synthetic Indian e-commerce dataset using cleaned customer, product, order and order-item tables. Revenue and profit are calculated at line level, with each order's discount percentage applied to its item lines. KPI reporting focuses on delivered orders unless stated otherwise.

- Delivered profit: **₹410,376,550.12**
- Profit margin: **30.18%**

## Important limitation
The dataset is synthetic. These figures demonstrate an analytics workflow and must not be represented as actual company performance or client results.

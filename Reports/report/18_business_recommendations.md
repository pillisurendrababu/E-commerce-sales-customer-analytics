# Business Recommendations

1. Prioritize categories using both revenue and profit, not revenue alone.
2. Review product-level margin before funding promotions or inventory expansion.
3. Use RFM segments to design differentiated retention and reactivation campaigns.
4. Investigate state-level logistics, returns and acquisition costs before geographic expansion.
5. Monitor payment success and failure rates alongside payment revenue.
6. Test discount changes with controlled experiments rather than assuming higher discounts create incremental sales.
7. Build monthly KPI monitoring to detect shifts in revenue, margin and order volume.

All recommendations are hypotheses based on synthetic descriptive data.

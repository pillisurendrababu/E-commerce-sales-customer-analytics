# Limitations and Assumptions

- All records are synthetic and do not describe a real retailer or actual customers.
- Revenue uses quantity × unit price less the order-level discount percentage.
- Cost uses quantity × product cost price.
- Main revenue/profit KPIs include delivered orders only.
- No shipping, tax, refunds, advertising spend, payment fees or returns cost fields are available.
- RFM scores use quintiles and simplified rule-based labels.
- Descriptive relationships do not establish causation.
- Results should be recalculated if source CSVs are changed.

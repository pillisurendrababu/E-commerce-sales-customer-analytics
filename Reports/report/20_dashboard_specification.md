# Power BI Dashboard Specification

## Page 1: Sales Overview
Cards: delivered revenue, delivered profit, profit margin, delivered orders, average order value. Visuals: monthly revenue trend, revenue and profit by category, order status distribution.

## Page 2: Product and Customer Performance
Visuals: top products by revenue and profit, units sold by category, customer RFM segment distribution, top customers by revenue.

## Page 3: Geographic and Payment Insights
Visuals: revenue by state and city, revenue by payment method, revenue by age group and gender.

## Suggested slicers
Order date, category, state, payment method and order status. Use delivered-only measures for the main sales KPIs.

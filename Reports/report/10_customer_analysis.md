# Customer Analysis

There are **9,416** unique customers with at least one delivered purchase. Customer-level revenue, order frequency and last purchase date are available in `tables/customer_performance.csv` when generated by the analysis scripts; RFM-level detail is provided in `tables/customer_rfm_segments.csv`.

Customer-level summaries are descriptive and should be paired with cohort or retention analysis before making claims about long-term loyalty.

# RFM Customer Segmentation

RFM segmentation uses:
- **Recency:** days since the customer's most recent delivered order.
- **Frequency:** number of delivered orders.
- **Monetary:** total delivered revenue.

Customers receive quintile-based R, F and M scores. Segment labels are rule-based and illustrative, not universal definitions. See `tables/customer_rfm_segments.csv`, `tables/customer_segment_summary.csv` and `charts/rfm_segments.png`.
